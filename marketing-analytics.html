Marketing Performance Analytics

Data-Driven Marketing Analytics for Global Hotel Brands Across APAC and EMEA

What is Marketing Analytics?

Marketing Performance Analytics is for international hotel chains across the APAC and EMEA regions, consolidating annual email marketing data to evaluate campaign effectiveness, monitor customer acquisition, measure marketing ROI, and identify growth opportunities. The platform provided interactive dashboards and performance insights to support data-driven marketing decisions across global markets. marketing ROI, and identify growth opportunities through data-driven insights.


Marketing Analytics is the process of collecting, measuring, and analyzing marketing data to evaluate campaign performance, understand customer behavior, optimize marketing investments, and improve business outcomes.

Business Questions Addressed

  • What is the customer conversion rate?
  • Which campaigns deliver the highest ROI?
  • What is the cost per lead and acquisition?
  • Which audience segments engage the most?
  • How effective are digital marketing efforts?
