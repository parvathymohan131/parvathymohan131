Sales Analytics Dashboard

Developed an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard to monitor revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and sales performance. The solution enables business stakeholders to make data-driven decisions through real-time reporting and actionable insights.

What is a Sales Dashboard?

A Sales Dashboard is a business intelligence solution that visualizes sales performance metrics and KPIs. It helps organizations monitor revenue trends, evaluate sales performance, identify opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.


  • Track revenue and profitability
  • Monitor sales performance
  • Analyze customer purchasing behavior
  • Identify top-performing products
  • Measure regional sales performance
  • Support forecasting and planning

Statistical Measures Used

Mean (Average)

Represents the average sales value.

Mean = Total Sales ÷ Number of Transactions

Business Use: Average Order Value, Monthly Sales Performance

Median

The middle value when sales data is sorted.

1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000 Median = 2000

Business Use: Typical Customer Spending Analysis

Mode

The most frequently occurring value.

1000, 1000, 1500, 2000 Mode = 1000

Business Use: Most Common Purchase Value
